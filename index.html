All values are calculated in real time as you enter data. The final deduction is rounded to the nearest tenth of a point.

Variables

RR — Rejection Rate. The share of an employee's completed accounts that Rejections ÷ Accounts.

T — Threshold. The rejection rate below which no points are deducted at all, since a small number of errors shouldn't be punished.

M — Max Rate. The rejection rate at which the rate-based deduction reaches its full severity.

k — Curve Exponent. Controls how sharply the deduction increases as the rejection rate moves from T toward M — a gentle ramp or a steep one. Set automatically by the selected Account Type and Role.

N — Total Accounts. The number of accounts the employee completed that month.

VF — Volume Floor. The number of accounts needed before the rejection rate is treated as fully trustworthy. Below this, the deduction is scaled down, since a small sample of work shouldn't be judged as harshly as a large one.

Formula

Final Deduction = (((RR − T) / (M − T))^k) × 5 × (N / VF)

RR is compared against T and M to find how far along the severity scale the employee sits, that position is shaped by the curve (k), scaled to the 5-point maximum, then adjusted down if the employee's monthly volume (N) falls short of the trusted volume floor (VF). The result is always bounded between 0 and 5.

Rejection Cap Override

Regardless of the formula above, logging 5 or more rejections in a single month automatically applies the full maximum deduction (5 points) — the rate-based calculation, threshold, curve, and volume floor are all bypassed once this cap is reached.
